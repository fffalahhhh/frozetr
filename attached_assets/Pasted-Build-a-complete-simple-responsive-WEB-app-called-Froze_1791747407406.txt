Build a complete, simple, responsive WEB app called “Frozen Shake Cash Tracker” for tracking the daily cash of my frozen shake shop. It should work well on a phone browser as well as desktop.

Keep it focused on the shop’s cash tracking. Don’t add login, cloud sync, inventory management, or complicated accounting features.

DATA AND CURRENCY
- Show amounts in Indian rupees (₹ / INR).
- Use the Asia/Kolkata local date when creating today’s records.
- Save data in this browser so it persists after refresh. Clearly tell me that the records are stored only in this browser and are not synced to other devices.
- Do not fill the app with fake sample transactions.
- If I attach the Frozen Shake logo, use it in the app branding. Keep the visual style simple, warm, and easy to read.

TODAY’S CASH
Create a Today screen where I can:
- Select a date and enter or update one total daily cash revenue amount for that date.
- Add multiple expenses for that date. Each expense has an amount, category, and optional note.
- Record cash handed to a partner. Each handover has a partner, amount, and optional note.
- Edit or delete an expense or handover, with confirmation before deleting.
- See the selected day’s revenue, total expenses, total handed to partners, and cash in hand.

Calculate:
Cash in hand = revenue − expenses − partner handovers.
A partner handover is cash leaving the shop; don’t count it again as an expense.

EXPENSE CATEGORIES
Start with these categories:
- Shop Stock
- Shop Utilities
- Salaries
- Advance

Let me add categories and manage them later. If a category is removed or hidden, don’t break or erase old expense records that used it.

PARTNERS
Start with these partners:
- Falah
- Kais
- Hussain
- Raees
- Alkama

Let me record each handover against the partner who received the cash. Show totals per partner and a recent handover list. The partner names are recipients, not app login accounts.

HISTORY
Create a History screen that lists days with recorded activity and shows each day’s revenue, expenses, handovers, and cash in hand. Let me open a past date and edit its records.

DESIGN AND QUALITY
- Keep navigation simple and make the Today screen the main screen.
- Use clear labels, large touch-friendly controls, and useful empty states.
- Validate amounts; don’t accept negative values or silently save invalid entries.
- Make sure adding, editing, deleting, date selection, calculations, and saving after refresh all work end to end.
- Keep the app straightforward and avoid features outside this scope.
